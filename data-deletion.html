Request deletion

Contact the application operator using the support details below. Use the subject “WhatsApp data deletion request”. Include your application username and linked WhatsApp number with country code, and state whether you want channel data removed or also want your web account and trade-book data reviewed for deletion. Never send passwords, access tokens or verification codes.

The operator will verify account ownership through the existing signed-in account or another agreed verification method before acting. Requests are handled manually; this page does not automatically submit a request. Ask support for confirmation of completion and details of any records that must be retained and why.

Stop access immediately

Open the WhatsApp linking page, sign in and choose Unlink, or send STOP to the bot. This revokes the link and invalidates conversation access. It does not immediately erase retained messages, your web account, trade books or audit history.

Scope and limitations

Channel data includes stored messages, delivery records, linking requests and contact associations. Regular cleanup is described in the privacy policy. Removing channel data does not remove messages from your device or Meta/WhatsApp systems. Hosting/database backup copies require separate operator handling. Support will explain any applicable retention limits for your request. No automatic Meta deletion callback is implemented.
